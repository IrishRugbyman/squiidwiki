# 752 / PJC and 56 - Instagram sweep, 2026-08-24

Two numbers that came out of `raw/sets.txt` line 24:

    TankMafia (AOG/264)x(PJC/752)♿️4️⃣

Both were chased through Instagram Business Discovery (see `~/squiidape-ig/CLAUDE.md`).
That API reads professional accounts only; personal accounts return "Invalid user id",
which is most of the ones below. Where an account was unreachable, the post pages
themselves still render to a logged-out fetch, so a permalink is worth more than a
profile URL when handing over a lead.

That gets you the pictures too, which matters more than it sounds. A personal account's
post page carries the `scontent-*.cdninstagram.com` URLs for its carousel items, and those
CDN objects download with a plain unauthenticated GET. So for an account the API refuses,
the route is: permalink -> page source -> image URL -> download -> read the frame. The
funeral programme, the casket wrap and the 752/264 graffiti in this file all came out
that way. Profile URLs stay useless; they serve a login wall.

## Photos, and why most of these men have none

Attempted for every member added here. Two got one, and the reason the others did not is
worth writing down so nobody repeats the attempt.

**Profile pictures are close to useless on these accounts.** Business Discovery hands them
over at 1080x1080, so resolution is not the problem; content is. `2lilmanny` and
`flashy_tank` use the **same photograph** as each other, of two men, with nothing to say
which of the two is which account. `56ongo` is shot from behind, facing a wall.
`tezghost7` is a pile of cash on a floor. `eastdav.zell` is a sideways two-person frame.
`whoisvoski` is two men and one of them is masked. Not one is a usable portrait.

**Vo's own feed does not solve it.** Thirty-five frames across fifteen posts, and in
nearly every one the subject's face is behind his hands, a mask, a hood or a fan of money.
That is deliberate, and it means there is no frame I can point at and say "this is Vo"
rather than "this is one of the men Vo posts". A wrong face on a member page is worse
than an empty one, so he has none.

What did work, both from the permalink route rather than the API:

- **C Deuce**, two. The casket wrap portrait is the primary; the funeral programme spread
  is the second. Both carry his name in frame, so there is no attribution question.
- **Ski**, one, cropped from his own 22 June 2026 post. Solo, face clear, 752 pendant
  visible, and it is the same frame as the 752/264 graffiti.

The pattern to take forward: **a memorial gives you a face, a feed usually does not.**
Funeral programmes, casket wraps and airbrushed jackets put the name and the face in the
same frame, which is exactly what a member row wants and what a live account withholds.

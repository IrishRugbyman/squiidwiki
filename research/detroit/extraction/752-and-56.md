# 752 / PJC and 56 - Instagram sweep, 2026-08-24

Two numbers that came out of `raw/sets.txt` line 24:

    TankMafia (AOG/264)x(PJC/752)♿️4️⃣

Both were chased through Instagram Business Discovery (see `~/squiidape-ig/CLAUDE.md`).
That API reads professional accounts only; personal accounts return "Invalid user id",
which is most of the ones below. Where an account was unreachable, the post pages
themselves still render to a logged-out fetch, so a permalink is worth more than a
profile URL when handing over a lead.

That gets you the pictures too, which matters more than it sounds. A personal account's
post page carries the `scontent-*.cdninstagram.com` URLs for its carousel items, and those
CDN objects download with a plain unauthenticated GET. So for an account the API refuses,
the route is: permalink -> page source -> image URL -> download -> read the frame. The
funeral programme, the casket wrap and the 752/264 graffiti in this file all came out
that way. Profile URLs stay useless; they serve a login wall.

### Dionna, and why Dion was on 1000Gang

**Dionna Janae Davis, 18**, was shot dead alongside **Dazhane Jasmine Holloway, 19**,
on **22 January 2021 at 6:35 p.m.** outside their home at the Knottingham Apartments on
Wellington Crescent, Clinton Township. The two of them were unloading groceries when two
men got out of a car across the parking lot and fired close to 40 rounds from two guns.
A child at the scene was unharmed.

**Darrell Semaj Banks**, 19, of Mount Clemens and **Dajuan Dion-Diego Davenport**, 20,
of Warren fled in a Dodge Charger and crashed during the pursuit at Eight Mile and
Gratiot on the Detroit-Eastpointe line; both were arrested that night. A Macomb County
jury convicted them in March 2023 on two counts of first-degree murder and felony
firearm, and both were sentenced to life without parole on 19 April 2023.

**Both men are 1000Gang.** The press never says so; that is street sourcing. It is also
the piece that makes the rest of the file read straight: Dion of 700 loses his sister to
two 1000Gang men in January 2021, and Chopsticc of 1000Gang is dead by Dion's hand.

One of the two is called **Twin** and the other name is not recovered yet, so neither
carries a nickname in the DB. Note that the wiki already holds an unrelated Twin
(Martaze Davis, Hustle Boyz).

Sources: ClickOnDetroit 25 Jan 2021 (charging) and 20 Apr 2023 (sentencing). The
Detroit News piece on the same case is paywalled to this server.

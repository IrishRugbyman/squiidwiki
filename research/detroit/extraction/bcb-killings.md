## Walvon Holland, 13 June 2007

**The court record replaced a street account here, and the two do not agree.**

His biography read: killed by BCB's older generation, the men holding the territory, shot
with a shotgun at his door after a brick came through his window. What the trial testimony
says is different in every particular.

**Source.** *Herbert v Rivard*, No. 2:11-cv-12967 (E.D. Mich., 27 Apr 2015), Friedman, J.,
denying habeas - read from **govinfo**, because law.justia.com returns 403 to this server
as `README.md` warns. It quotes the Michigan Court of Appeals' recitation of the trial
testimony in *People v Herbert*, No. 284313, 2009 WL 1682140 (Mich Ct App, 16 June 2009).
Trial court: *People v Herbert*, No. 07-11024-01, Wayne County Circuit Court. Rated **HIGH**.

> Defendant's convictions arise from the shooting death of 16-year-old Walvon Holland, who
> was shot three times in the head and once in the chest while inside the basement bedroom
> of his friend, Kurtis Kelm.

What the testimony establishes:

- Holland and **Kurtis Kelm** sold marijuana together and kept the supply in Kelm's
  basement bedroom.
- Kelm was asleep there and woke to **Rodgereck Herbert** demanding drugs and money from
  Holland. From a partly concealed spot he saw Herbert point a gun, and saw him fire when
  Holland refused.
- **A second man Kelm never saw** came down; the drugs and money were gone once both left.
- Bench trial. Herbert convicted of first-degree felony murder and felony firearm,
  sentenced **20 February 2008** to life plus two years.

**No gang, no shotgun and no brick appear anywhere in the opinion**, and the words are not
in it at all. The old account is parked unattached in `leads.md`; it is not Beezy's either,
whose death is settled below.

**MDOC 615989, Rodgereck Herbert.** Born 1 October 1988, so **18** against Holland's 16.
Felony murder LIFE and felony firearm 2 years on court file 07011024-01-FC, both sentenced
20 February 2008. Carson City Correctional Facility, level II. SID 2739772L. Street names
**Bama** and **Bookie** - real ones, so unlike the OTIS legal-name spellings these went
into `aliases`. Left forearm tattoo, "Money Over Everything".

**The homicide list garbles him as "Wolvon Howell", 16, 18997 Lenore.** Same date, same
age; the court record settles that it is Walvon Holland, so the address is used. It
geocodes to 42.427503, -83.281793 - Five Points, ZIP 48219, but roughly 2 km west of the
Burgess/Chapel/Blackstone cluster and on the far side of Lahser. Note the address is
almost certainly **Kelm's** house, not his.

## Beezy is Rodney Autrey, 26 October 2010

Beezy was on file as BCB and DEAD, dated 26 October 2010, with no legal name and no
incident. The 2010 homicide list holds four Detroit killings that day:

| **Rodney Autrey** | 22 | **21435 W. 8 Mile** |

**The address decides it.** 21435 W 8 Mile Rd geocodes to 42.443292, -83.254357, and from
that point:

    Chapel Street       68 m
    Burgess Court      205 m
    Blackstone Court   257 m
    Lahser Road        370 m

Every street in BCB's own name is within 370 m, and Chapel is essentially at the corner. Of
the four, only Autrey is both young and there. **The user confirmed the identification from
a photograph.**

**And the alliance is named for the day.** BCB sits in **1026Family**, alias *BeezyBoyz*.
10/26 is the date Beezy died. That went into the alliance description, which was empty and
is the only field that can hold it.

**Keylen Philyaw** was killed at the same address on the same day and is seeded as a
member, `DEAD`, parked in Unknown - whether he was BCB is not established.

Neither man is in OTIS, so neither had a state record, and no press account of the killing
was found. Nobody is attributed to it, and no set participant was written.

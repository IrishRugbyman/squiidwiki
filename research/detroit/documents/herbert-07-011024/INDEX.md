# People v Herbert - Wayne Circuit LC No. 07-011024-01-FC

The killing of Walvon Holland, 13 June 2007, 18997 Lenore Ave. Wiki members
`Rodgereck Herbert` (`rodgereck-herbert`, MDOC 615989) and `Walvon` (`walvon`).
The trial record is written up in `../../extraction/bcb-killings.md`; this
directory holds the convicted man's own account.

## Documents

Three photographed pages of a typed prison manuscript, autobiography in
chapters, opening with a preface that declares innocence. Not a court filing,
so the `YYYY-MM-DD-<court>-<docket>` naming does not apply; files are named by
manuscript page, which is what they are numbered by at the foot.

## What it establishes

Self-identification is unambiguous: "My name is Rodgereck Markeith Herbert.
I'm inmate# 615989." That ties the manuscript to the OTIS record already held,
and gives the **middle name Markeith**, which OTIS does not carry.

**He maintains he is innocent** and calls the conviction unconstitutional,
citing the Sixth and Fourteenth Amendments and due process. He states the trial
ran **seven days** and was a **bench trial** - the bench trial is confirmed by
the Court of Appeals recitation; the seven-day length is his figure alone.

**He and Holland were close friends.** "My most dearest and beloved friend...
I owe Wolvon my life." The trial testimony has him robbing Holland at gunpoint
in Kelm's basement; his account has the dead man as his closest friend and
someone else as the killer, addressed directly: "You know, the killer knows,
God knows and I know your killer wasn't me."

**"Bookie" is the Georgiana name, and it is a family name, not a street one**:
"In Georgiana, Alabama I was known as 'Bookie' by family and friends." Both
`Bookie` and `Bama` are already in his `aliases`; this settles where each came
from.

## Biography carried by Chapters I and II

- Born and raised in **Georgiana, Alabama** - a one-grocery-store town, better
  known as the birthplace of Hank Williams Sr.
- Moved to Detroit with his mother **around November 2001**, so at **13**.
- They lived first with his great-aunt **Pauline Littles** (known as Sergeant
  Major Littles) and her husband **Donald Littles** (Deacon Littles), the
  closest thing to a father he names, then took an apartment of their own.
- His mother's three children: **Marlon**, the oldest, eight years his senior,
  a school dropout who "decided to dropout of school and lead a life of crime"
  and was repeatedly in trouble with the law; **Ladessa**, the middle child, who
  also quit school; and Rodgereck, the baby. He looked up to Marlon and writes
  that he was conflicted about following him.
- Great-grandmother **Laura Mae**; grandmother **Ludester**, Pauline's sister.
- **Father absent** - met once that he can remember, never helped raise him.
- **Failed the seventh grade** in Georgiana; the failure is why his mother moved
  him. In Detroit he enrolled at **Langston Hughes Academy Middle School**, got
  past seventh and eighth grade, and finished middle school - he calls the
  graduation ceremony the proudest moment of his life, then and now.
- Best friend in Georgiana was **E.J.**, whom he describes as a twin brother.

## The spelling of Walvon's first name

The manuscript writes **Wolvon Holland**, three times, from a man who says he
was his closest friend. The 2007 homicide list also has **Wolvon** (under the
garbled surname "Howell"). Only the Court of Appeals opinion in
*People v Herbert* has **Walvon**, and the wiki follows it. Two independent
non-court sources against one court source; unresolved, flagged in
`../../extraction/bcb-killings.md`.
